Meet the Real Rise

A Companion for Artemis II

The Artemis II crew selected Rise as the mission's zero-gravity indicator.

What Is a Zero-Gravity Indicator?

It is a small object, often a plush toy, that floats freely when the spacecraft and its contents experience weightlessness. It provides a simple visual cue for the crew.

Designed by Lucas Ye

Rise was designed by Lucas Ye from Mountain View, California.

Inspired by Earthrise

The design pays tribute to Earthrise, the iconic view of Earth above the lunar horizon associated with Apollo 8.

Our Interactive Adaptation

Astro_Bytes uses an illustrated adaptation of Rise as a playful virtual companion. The expressions, coin game, and accessories belong to this independent prototype, not to an official NASA application.
